Who we are

A student design.
A tested toboggan.

We are GNCTR Club at NAIT, a student-led team building a toboggan for the Great Northern Concrete Toboggan Race in Edmonton. We apply classroom learning to real engineering: concrete running surfaces, structural systems, steering and brakes that all have to work together on snow.
