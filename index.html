GNCTR Club at NAIT · Edmonton, Alberta

We pour concrete and race it down a hill.

We’re a team of engineering technology students from across NAIT’s programs, competing in GNCTR, a national design-and-build competition held annually. This year it’s in Edmonton, and our team brings together students from a range of disciplines who’ve competed together before and are now building for 2027.

We’ll design and build the toboggan ourselves, present it at technical exhibitions, and race it against institutions from across Canada. GNCTR is a tradition among engineering schools, a chance to show technical ability, design skill, and procurement, and it has drawn national media coverage.

Meet the team

Students who get their hands dirty.

We are a student-led team. We choose the materials, weigh the mix, build the toboggan and test it. Then we explain every design decision at the event.

What the team does

Design

We plan the toboggan, choose a concrete mix, and work out how it will steer and stop.

Build

Members cast, fabricate and test the toboggan, then fix whatever the tests find.

Race

We take the finished toboggan to the event and race it against teams from other schools.

Letter of support

Why a letter helps

A short letter from a company, instructor, graduate or community member shows sponsors and funders that people stand behind the team.

Use the template as a starting point. Change the bracketed parts so it says what you actually know about the team, then send it to an executive member or message us on Instagram.

Template letter

[Date]

To whom it may concern,

I am writing on behalf of [organization or yourself] to support GNCTR Club at NAIT, a student club competing in the Great Northern Concrete Toboggan Race (GNCTR).

[Describe your connection to the club or to NAIT. For example: we have hired NAIT graduates, we have worked with these students, or we have seen the team's work.]

The team gives students hands-on experience in design, concrete materials, fabrication, teamwork and fundraising. These skills matter in industry. [Add one or two sentences on why this matters to your organization.]

I encourage you to support this team.

Sincerely,
