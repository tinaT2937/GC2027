Why partner with us
  • Meet emerging talent. Students building design, project management, fabrication and communication skills.
  • Put your brand on the build. Toboggan, team channels, exhibition and selected materials.
  • Support applied learning. From calculations and drawings to fabrication, testing and safety review.
  • See your support in action. Your contribution becomes a working toboggan.

Other ways to help
  • Materials. Concrete products, reinforcement, steel, fasteners, coatings and consumables.
  • Fabrication. Specialized trade support such as machining and welding, within competition rules.
  • Testing & safety. Inspection support, PPE, transport, test-site access and feedback.
  • Advice. General engineering theory only. Students keep responsibility for design decisions and drawings.
