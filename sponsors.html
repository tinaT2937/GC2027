Other ways to help

  1. Materials

    Concrete products, reinforcement, steel, fasteners, coatings and fabrication consumables.

  2. Fabrication

    Specialized trade support such as machining and welding, subject to competition rules.

  3. Testing & safety

    Inspection support, PPE, transportation, test-site access and performance feedback.

  4. High-level theoretical advice

    General engineering theory only. Students keep responsibility for design decisions and drawings.
